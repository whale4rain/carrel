5.1 HOW BUYERS RESPOND

Elasticity describes how strongly buyers react when a price moves. When a small price change shifts the quantity bought a lot, demand is elastic; when buyers barely react, it is inelastic.

Goods with close substitutes tend to be elastic. Habits and necessities tend to be inelastic, because there is little else to switch to.

Time matters too. Over a week, drivers keep buying fuel at almost any price; over several years, they find other ways to travel.

Elastic = buyers react strongly. p.42

Elasticity describes how strongly buyers react when a price moves. When a small price change shifts the quantity bought a lot, demand is elastic.

1Habits and necessities tend to be inelastic, because there is little else to switch to.

2Time matters too. Over a week, drivers keep buying fuel at almost any price; over several years, they find other ways to travel.

The share of income a good takes also matters: people notice price changes on large purchases more.
